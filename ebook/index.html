01About the publication

From Regulation to Practical Implementation

Move from knowing a DPIA is required to conducting one properly.

Data Privacy Impact Assessments are a critical part of responsible data governance, yet many organisations still struggle with the practical question:

How do we move from knowing that a DPIA is required to conducting one properly, documenting it clearly and using it to support real decisions?

DPIA Made Easy – Professional Edition was developed to bridge that gap.

The publication provides a structured, practical and governance-focused approach to DPIA implementation, helping organisations and professionals identify high-risk processing, assess privacy risks, develop proportionate mitigation measures and demonstrate accountability.

S. A. Kitchener, CIPP/E, CIPM is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of ACE Data Protection Consulting Ltd, a Data Protection Compliance Organisation (DPCO) licensed by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

She has completed Executive Education in Data Protection & Technology at Harvard Business School and brings extensive experience supporting organisations across the public and private sectors within Europe, the Middle East and Africa (EMEA).

She has worked with organisations at various stages of data protection maturity, guiding the implementation of regulatory frameworks and developing practical, business-focused compliance programmes.

Through this work, she identified a consistent gap between regulatory expectations and real-world implementation— an insight that inspired the development of DPIA Made Easy – Professional Edition.

Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before the Professional Edition launches.

Is DPIA Made Easy suitable for beginners?

Yes. The publication is structured progressively and is designed to support both professionals new to DPIAs and experienced practitioners seeking a more structured implementation approach.

Is the publication aligned with the Nigeria Data Protection Act?

Yes. It is primarily aligned with the NDPA and supported by relevant regulatory guidance and recognised international good practice.

Does the workbook include practical exercises?

Yes. It includes reflection exercises, case studies, checklists and practical implementation guidance.

Is the ACE DPIA Toolkit included?

The Toolkit is a separate companion resource within the ACE DPIA Professional Series.

Can ACE provide corporate training?

Yes. Corporate, practitioner and executive DPIA training options will be available.

Can ACE help us conduct a DPIA?

Yes. Organisations can request advisory and implementation support directly through ACE Data Protection Consulting.
